## 1. Structure — bolted outriggers to chassis (not GI skin)

- Primary load path: **bolted outriggers / subframe to bed rails / chassis rails**.
- Do **not** hang shell mass from dropside GI sheet metal alone.
- Prefer through-bolts with backing plates; isolate dissimilar metals; seal penetrations.
- Floor pan (P1) lands on outriggers; walls transfer to floor / corner posts, not skin.

## 2. Landing legs when occupied

- Provide hard points for **landing / stabilizer legs** when the vehicle is occupied or parked extended.
- Legs take occupancy / overhang moment off the rear suspension when static.
- Do not rely on stock springs alone for long-term loft loading at camp.

## 3. Max-kg placard (post-weigh)

- After shell is built and mounted, **weigh as-built** (axle scales preferred).
- Affix **MAX kg** placard (GVW / axle / payload remaining) inside entry and on build plate.
- Recalculate payload vs TMP LWB rating (~1025–1110 kg ref — verify VIN).

## 4. Splash / drain / weep

- Floor perimeter: weep holes / drain paths so water does not pond on pan.
- Over-cab / roof junctions: reverse-lap or gasket; no blind pockets.
- Door sill and window pans: drain outward; no inward weep into loft.

## 5. As-found freeze

- Tape-measure and photograph: bed L×W, rail spacing, cab roof H, FOH/ROH, VIN plate.
- Freeze as-found dims on a shop traveler before cut list release.
- OAL ≈6300 / floor 3647×1911 are **Architect CONDITIONAL** — confirm on this vehicle.

## Scope IN (Phase-1)

1. Weatherproof box on bolted outriggers to bed rails (not GI skin alone).
2. Over-cab loft / room (~1223 mm).
3. Continuous roof (OAH ≤ 2500 mm).
4. Openings: entry door, 2 body windows, over-cab window (optional front-face per SVG).
5. Splash / drain / weep paths; landing-leg hard points when occupied.
6. Max-kg placard after as-built weigh.

## Scope OUT

- Interior fit-out, cabinetry, upholstery  
- Wet systems (fresh / grey / black) beyond drain/weep placeholders  
- Electrical / solar / LPG beyond shell penetrations as placeholders  
- LTO certification, weight certification, road-worthiness paperwork

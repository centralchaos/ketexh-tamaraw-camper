## 1. Structure — bolted outriggers, not GI skin

- Primary load path: bolted outriggers to the bed rails.
- Do not hang the shell from dropside sheet metal alone.
- Through-bolts with backing plates. Isolate dissimilar metals. Seal penetrations.
- Welds are inside the shell-module weldment only. Do not weld to the chassis rail.
- Plate size, hole diameter, quantity, grade, and spacing are **TBD** on D-01.
- **No undirected rail drill.** The Jay GO box on D-01 is unsigned.

## 2. Landing legs when occupied

- Hard points under the +1000 tail. See D-02.
- Occupied only on stands. Do not leave habitation load on the stock springs.
- Foot pad, pin, and swing clearance are **TBD**. Confirm on this vehicle.
- Leg holes are shell-module holes, not a rail drill.

## 3. Max-kg placard

- After the shell is mounted, weigh it (axle scales).
- Only then letter the MAX kg placard inside the entry and on the build plate.
- D-02 draws the placard **blank**. Do not write a number on this revision.

## 4. Splash, drain, weep, hose

- Bed-to-tail sill: seal, outward weep, splash box that daylights. See D-03.
- Cab-to-over-cab joint: reverse lap or gasket. No blind pocket.
- 10 minute hose at each of those joints. Pressure TBD.
- FAIL-IF wet cabin, trapped water, or wet glands.

## 5. As-found freeze

Tape and photograph bed length and width, rail spacing, cab roof height, overhangs, and the VIN plate before a cut list. Overall length ≈ 6300 and floor 3647 × 1911 are Architect CONDITIONAL.

## 6. Roof hatch and lounge frame (D-04)

- The lounge opening is a framed hole with a continuous seal to the shell. It is not a cut in the skin alone.
- Hinge and strut hardpoints land on that frame. Rail posts land in the roof rails or sills.
- Opening size, sealant, and weld size are **TBD**.
- Occupied roof is parked only. Close the hatch and raise the rail before driving.
- OAH ≤ 2500 is the hatch-closed height.
- Hose the closed perimeter before any parked-lounge claim.
- FAIL-IF the cut is open with no sealed frame, the rail is missing, water enters the lounge, or the hatch is open on the road.

## 7. Wall ladder (D-05)

- Bolts into the rear posts or sills, on stand-offs. Not skin-only.
- Grade, quantity, and spacing are **TBD**. Not a chassis-rail drill.
- The ladder is roof access. It is not a jack and not a shell lift.
- FAIL-IF it is used to lift the shell, or the mounts loosen after vibration.

## 8. Bumper and splash (D-06)

- Bumper meets the shell sill under the +1000 tail.
- Keep the D-03 belly splash and weep. Do not dam them.
- Hitch must clear the landing-leg swing. The gap is **TBD**.
- Lamp and mudflap glands stay sealed. Product TBD.
- FAIL-IF water is trapped under the occupied tail, or glands are wet at this interface.
